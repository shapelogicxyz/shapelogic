---
title: User guide
description: How to use ShapeLogic to design and solve puzzles.
---

ShapeLogic designs and solves puzzles made of voxel pieces: burrs and other
interlocking puzzles, packing puzzles, sliding puzzles and stacking puzzles.

New here? [Install](/docs/install/) the app, then follow
[Getting started](/docs/getting-started/) for a tour of the three workspaces
and a first solve.

:::note
This guide is new and still growing. It follows the old
[BurrTools user guide](https://burrtools.sourceforge.net/gui-doc/toc.html)
in spirit, since the ideas carry over, but the screens and wording are
ShapeLogic's own.
:::
