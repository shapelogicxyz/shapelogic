title: User Guide
description: How to use ShapeLogic to design and solve puzzles.
---

ShapeLogic designs and solves puzzles made of voxel pieces: burrs and other
interlocking puzzles, packing puzzles, sliding puzzles and stacking puzzles.

New here? [Install](/docs/install/) the app, then follow
[Getting started](/docs/getting-started/) for a tour of the three workspaces
and a first solve.

Then read about the [puzzle types](/docs/puzzle-types/): Brick, Sliding,
Stacking, Triangular Prism, Spheres, Rhombic and Tetrahedra-Octahedra.

## Using ShapeLogic

- [3D Model Viewer](/docs/viewer/): moving around the 3D view, the Display
  menu's styles, and the view cube.
- [Shapes Tab Options](/docs/shapes-tab/), [Puzzle Tab Options](/docs/puzzle-tab/)
  and [Solver Tab Options](/docs/solver-tab/): every button and option of the
  three workspaces.
- [Keyboard Shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts/) for Windows, Linux and
  macOS.
- [Imports](/docs/imports/) and [Exports](/docs/exports/): other programs'
  puzzle files, images, STL files for 3D printing, and animations.

## Advanced Topics

- [Color Restrictions](/docs/color-restrictions/): limit where pieces may go,
  and what Strict Color Restrictions changes.
- [Piece Rotations (TIC)](/docs/piece-rotations/): puzzles where pieces must
  turn, and the two solver types.
- [Solve Notation](/docs/solve-notation/): assemblies, solutions, levels and
  the dotted level code.
- [Command Line Tool](/docs/command-line/): solve puzzles from a terminal or
  a script.

:::note
This guide is new and still growing. It follows the old
[BurrTools user guide](https://burrtools.sourceforge.net/gui-doc/toc.html)
in spirit, since the ideas carry over, but the screens and wording are
ShapeLogic's own.
:::
